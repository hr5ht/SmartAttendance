Tips for a photo that passes
  • Be alone in the frame — only one face is allowed.
  • Hold the phone about an arm's length away, at eye level.
  • Face a window or a lamp. Don't stand with a bright light behind you.
  • Take off sunglasses, hats and masks. Glasses are fine.
  • Hold still until the camera focuses so the photo isn't blurry.
